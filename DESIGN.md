# RENTAMAQ PRO - INDUSTRIAL HEAVY EQUIPMENT DESIGN SYSTEM

## 1. Visual World: Industrial Heavy-Equipment Fleet (Authoritative & Tactical)
A bespoke design language inspired by heavy machinery operations, civil engineering yards, and industrial tool fleet management (Caterpillar, Hilti, Komatsu, United Rentals). Replaces soft generic AI-generated SaaS card layouts with a tactile, high-trust, engineered interface designed for contractors, project engineers, and machinery providers.

## 2. Typography
- **Display & Section Titles:** Barlow Condensed (700, 800, 900). Bold, compressed, high-impact, uppercase letterforms that convey heavy industrial equipment power.
- **Body & Controls:** Barlow (400, 500, 600, 700). High legibility under high-glare construction site conditions.
- **Data, Telemetry & SKUs:** JetBrains Mono (500, 700). Fixed-width technical numbers, serial numbers, timestamps, and currency values.

## 3. Color Palette
- **Carbon Base:** #0a0e17 (Deep Asphalt), #111625 (Industrial Slate), #1a2236 (Steel Dark).
- **Safety Amber / Caution:** #f59e0b (Machinery Gold), #fbbf24 (High-Vis Accent), #d97706 (Heavy Construction).
- **Operational Status:**
  - Operativo / En Patio: #059669 (Field Emerald)
  - En Faena / Reservado: #d97706 (Active Amber)
  - Mantenimiento / Taller: #475569 (Workshop Slate)
- **Neutral Light:** #f8fafc (Concrete White), #f1f5f9 (Light Aggregate), #e2e8f0 (Steel Border).

## 4. Elimination of "AI Slop" Anti-Patterns
- **No uniform bubbly cards:** Radii tuned to crisp industrial precision (6px - 12px) with engineered 1px hairline borders.
- **No generic icon-plus-text 4-column blocks:** Replaced with a unified Technical Operational Bar with live dispatch guarantees, RETIE compliance badges, and real contractor SLA metrics.
- **Deep Technical Information Density:** Tool cards prominently display real technical specs (Weight, Impact Joules, Voltage/Fuel, EPP level) and dual-tier pricing (Daily vs Weekly -15% saving) rather than generic marketing blurbs.
- **Bespoke Industrial Accents:** Safety hatching, tactile status LED indicators, physical hardware buttons, and dark industrial command panels.
